# ETL & ELT Study Materials

This study material provides a practical introduction to **ETL and ELT processes**, covering the complete journey of data from source systems to analytics and business intelligence platforms. It focuses on the concepts, tools, techniques, and hands-on practices required to understand modern data integration pipelines.

The material introduces popular ETL/ELT tools such as **Informatica, Qlik Talend, Fivetran, and Matillion**, along with the fundamentals of extracting data from different source systems, transforming and standardizing data, and loading it efficiently into target systems.

It also covers important **data integration and data quality concepts**, including data profiling, data cleansing, handling duplicates, managing missing values, validation, error handling, and pipeline logging. These concepts help learners understand how to build reliable and trustworthy data pipelines.

Along with the theoretical concepts, the study material is supported by practical labs covering **compute management**, **SQL querying**, and **data visualization**. Learners will work with data loading, transformation, validation, warehouse compute, analytical queries, and visualization to understand how transformed data ultimately supports reporting and BI use cases.

By completing these materials and labs, learners will gain a practical understanding of how modern data engineering pipelines are designed, executed, monitored, validated, and consumed for **analytics, dashboards, reporting, and business decision-making**.
